# External resources

- [Radio Garden](https://radio.garden/) — browse Monterrey, Nuevo León stations for ambient and focused listening.
- Seek northern Mexican talk radio, sports talk, interviews and local news; confirm station availability and program format before recommending specific broadcasts.
- Later broaden to speakers across Mexico and Latin America.

**Practice distinction:** Ambient radio can build familiarity; focused tasks require recall, replay, comprehension checks and review. No paid tutoring app required.
