# Vision and goals

Build an adaptive AI-assisted Spanish apprenticeship that turns previously studied grammar into practical listening comprehension and spontaneous conversation. The long-term speaking target is natural northern Mexican Spanish around Monterrey, motivated partly by professional contact with a Jabil manufacturing site that builds HVAC control boxes for Carrier. Everyday language takes precedence over technical manufacturing dialogues initially.

## Success measures
- Understand short, unfamiliar spoken passages without a transcript.
- Recognize and retrieve useful vocabulary in real speech.
- Respond spontaneously with increasing confidence.
- Eventually understand varied Latin American speakers and discuss everyday and workplace topics.

Fluency and a native-like accent are aspirational long-term goals, not promised outcomes or deadlines. Establish a baseline before setting measurable targets.
